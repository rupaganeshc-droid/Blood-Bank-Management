About Our Blood Bank

Our mission is to provide a reliable platform that bridges the gap between blood donors and those in urgent need. We simplify the donation and request process, ensuring timely help to save lives.

🩸 Who Can Donate Blood?

  • Age between 18 to 65 years
  • Minimum body weight of 50 kg
  • Hemoglobin count of at least 12.5 g/dL
  • Pulse rate between 60 to 100 without irregularities
  • Blood pressure within acceptable limits (e.g., 120/80 mmHg)

❌ Who Cannot Donate?

  • If you've had a recent infection or fever
  • If you've undergone surgery within the last 6 months
  • If you're pregnant or breastfeeding
  • If you've received a tattoo or piercing in the last 6 months
  • If you’re currently on antibiotics or steroids

💡 Why Donate Blood?

  • It saves lives during emergencies, surgeries, and cancer treatments
  • It helps your body by stimulating new blood cell production
  • Donating regularly can improve your health monitoring
  • You become part of a life-saving community

Be a hero in someone’s life. Donate blood. Save lives.
